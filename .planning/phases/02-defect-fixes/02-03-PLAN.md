<objective>
Close the two genuine FIX-08 gaps on the dashboard, close the FIX-06 caller defect at the dashboard's score tile, and lock FIX-04 and FIX-06 as automated contracts.

Purpose: FIX-08 is the requirement with real remaining work, not just verification. Two of the dashboard's data views answer a zero-data session dishonestly: the trend panel renders a loading skeleton that never resolves (so a dead device looks like a slow one) and the activity feed renders a bare line of muted text with no icon, no cause and no next action. FIX-04 is genuinely already correct in `StatusChip.jsx` and has zero automated coverage, so nothing would notice a regression. FIX-06 is correct in `BentoGrid.jsx` and *wrong at its caller*: the component owns the tone mapping, and `DashboardPage.jsx:321` maps the `safe` verdict to the sage `success` tone while the sibling score tile at `:351` passes the verdict's own value straight through, so a good reading wants the `safe` key that `ICON_TONES` does not have and silently renders `neutral` — a grey tile for a good foot, contradicting `DESIGN.md:51-56`. The component cannot detect this (it absorbs an unmapped key by design), so the fix belongs at the caller and the proof belongs in a rendered assertion that names the tone each verdict must produce.

Output: the dashboard's trend panel and activity feed brought to the three-state contract, the score tile's tone mapped to the metric's semantic colour, plus `DashboardPage.states.test.jsx`, `StatePanel.test.jsx`, `StatusChip.test.jsx` and `BentoGrid.test.jsx` locking FIX-08, FIX-04 and FIX-06.
</objective>
